Ludant Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Ludant iOS app handles information when you use it to discover and manage compatible Bluetooth controllers. Ludant does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Ludant stores saved controller records and unfinished module configuration drafts on your device so you can find controllers again and continue your work. These records can include a controller's Bluetooth identifier and name, firmware details, and module settings. Controller data and live sensor readings are used by the app; Ludant does not send raw sensor readings to a Ludant server. You can remove saved controllers in the app. Removing Ludant removes its local app data, subject to device backups managed by Apple.

Optional analytics

Analytics is off unless you choose to enable it in Ludant's Analytics and Privacy settings. If enabled, Ludant sends Google Analytics for Firebase events about app opens, controller discovery and connection outcomes, general controller and sensor types, module-request screen opens, and which visualisations are opened and for how long. Ludant's event details do not include your name, email address, Bluetooth identifier, controller name, or raw sensor readings. Firebase Analytics may also process an app-instance identifier, app and device details, session information, and approximate location for analytics. Ludant does not use analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can turn analytics off at any time in the app. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

External services

If you choose browser-based firmware setup, Ludant opens Espressif ESP Launchpad and firmware files hosted on GitHub Pages. Those services receive the network information needed to serve the pages and files, such as your IP address and browser information. Their privacy policies apply. You can choose not to use browser setup and use the firmware ZIP option instead.

If you choose to request a module, Ludant opens a Google Form. The form asks for details such as the module name, product link, interface or category, intended use, and notes. You may optionally provide an email address for follow-up. Information you submit is sent to Google and handled under Google's privacy terms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a module request.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local controller records and drafts remain on your device until you remove them or remove the app. Analytics retention is controlled by Google Analytics property settings and Google's retention practices. For a privacy or analytics-data deletion request, contact Ludant using the details below; we will review requests and delete information we can identify and control, subject to legal obligations and technical limits of third-party services. You can control Bluetooth access in iOS Settings and disable analytics in Ludant's settings.

Security and changes

Ludant uses iOS app storage for local records and relies on security controls provided by Apple, Google, GitHub, and Espressif for their services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated as Ludant's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
