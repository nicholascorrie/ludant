Ludant Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how the Ludant iOS app handles information when you use it to discover and manage compatible Bluetooth controllers. Ludant does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Ludant stores saved controller records and unfinished module configuration drafts on your device so you can find controllers again and continue your work. These records can include a controller's Bluetooth identifier and name, firmware details, and module settings. Controller data and live sensor readings are used by the app to provide its features; Ludant does not send raw sensor readings to a Ludant server. You can remove saved controllers in the app. Removing Ludant from your device removes its local app data, subject to any device backups managed by Apple.

Diagnostics and app analytics

When Firebase Analytics is configured for a build, Ludant sends Google Analytics for Firebase events about app use. These include app opens, controller discovery and connection outcomes, the general controller and sensor types used, module-request screen opens, and which visualisations are opened and for how long. The event details sent by Ludant do not include your name, email address, Bluetooth identifier, controller name, or raw sensor readings.

Google Analytics for Firebase also uses an app-instance identifier and may process app, device, operating-system, session, and approximate location information to provide analytics. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. Ludant uses analytics to understand feature use and improve the app; Ludant does not use these analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

External services

If you choose the browser-based firmware setup, Ludant opens Espressif ESP Launchpad and public firmware files hosted on GitHub Pages. Those services receive the network request information needed to serve the pages and files, such as your IP address and browser information. Their own privacy policies apply to that use. You can choose not to use the browser setup and use the firmware ZIP option instead.

The optional module-request form is not currently configured in the app. If it is enabled in a future release, the form will identify the information it asks you to submit, and that information will be sent to the form provider. Do not include sensitive personal information in a module request.

Retention and deletion

Local controller records and drafts remain on your device until you remove them or remove the app. Analytics retention is controlled by the Google Analytics property settings and Google's retention practices; aggregated reports may remain after event-level data expires. If you have a privacy or analytics-data deletion request, contact Ludant using the privacy contact details published with the app. We will review and respond to requests and delete information we can identify and control, subject to legal obligations and technical limits of third-party services.

Your choices

You can control Ludant's Bluetooth access in iOS Settings. You can stop using external firmware services at any time by closing the browser and not proceeding with the flash. This version does not provide a separate in-app switch for Firebase Analytics; analytics collection is active when Firebase is configured in the installed build.

Security and changes

Ludant uses iOS app storage for local records and relies on the security controls provided by Apple, Google, GitHub, and Espressif for their services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated as Ludant's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
